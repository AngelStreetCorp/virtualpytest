# The app in English, French and German

You can use the VirtualPyTest web app in English, French or German.

## Changing the language or the theme

- **On a computer:** open **Settings** in the top bar. At the bottom of the menu there are two rows:
  - **Theme**: light, dark or system (follows your computer). Hover an icon to see its name.
  - **Language**: `EN`, `FR` or `DE`.
- **On a phone:** tap **More** in the bottom bar. The same two rows are at the bottom of the menu.
- **On the login page:** the theme button at the top right opens the same two rows.

The app changes at once, without reloading the page. Your choice is remembered in this browser. On your first visit
the app uses your browser's language if it is one of the three, otherwise English.

## Reports open in the same language

A test report opened from the app (Test Reports, Run Tests, Monitoring, Heatmap) opens in the app's language. The
report has its own `EN | FR | DE` switch, and both share the same choice. See [Report languages](./report-languages.md).

## A link in a given language

To send someone a link that opens in a given language, add the language to the link: French, German or English. The app opens in that language and remembers it.

<div data-technical>

Add `?lang=fr` (or `de`, `en`) to an app link, for example `https://<your-server>/?lang=de`.

</div>

## What stays as it is

The app does not translate names and data: device, host, script, campaign and user names, logs, statuses sent by
devices, OCR text and error messages that come from the server. The documentation pages (`Docs`) are in English.

## Not everything is translated yet

The top bar, its menus, the phone bottom bar and the Dashboard are translated. The other pages are being translated
one by one, and until then they show English.
