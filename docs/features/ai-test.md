# AI Test Agent

**Describe what to test in plain English. The AI runs the device and tells you if it passed.**

You talk to your lab like a colleague. You do not write a script or click through a navigation tree. You describe what should happen. The AI agent operates the real device, checks the result, and shows you the evidence.

---

## Why it helps

Writing tests the usual way takes time and some technical skill.

- Writing and maintaining a script is slow work.
- Mapping every screen of an app by hand takes long before you can test anything.
- Non-technical people cannot easily ask "does this still work?" without a QA engineer.

---

## What you get

- **Test Prompt.** Describe what to do and how to know it passed. The AI navigates the real device, does the actions, and judges the result itself. You get screenshots as evidence.
- **Automatic app crawling.** Point the AI at an app. It explores on its own, finds the screens, and builds the navigation map for you.
- **Chat with the device.** Ask the AI to inspect, go to, or check something on a live device. You get an answer straight away.
- **Several device types.** Works on mobile, Android TV, set-top boxes and web apps.

---

## What it is good for

- Get a test running from one sentence.
- Map a new app's navigation without doing it screen by screen.
- Let anyone on the team ask real questions about a device's current state.
- Answer a one-off question, such as "does the settings menu still open?", in seconds.

---

## Where to find it

- **Test Prompt** page. Write your instruction and the pass conditions, pick a device, and run it.
- **Ask AI.** Available across the platform, to chat with a device directly.
