# 🎬 Audio & Video Quality Monitoring

**Not just "is it on" — "does it look and sound good."**

VirtualPyTest continuously scores the picture and sound quality of every monitored device, so quality problems are caught automatically instead of waiting for a customer complaint.

---

## The Problem

A device can look "up" and still be delivering a bad experience:
- ❌ Blurry, blocky or pixelated video that standard uptime checks don't notice
- ❌ Choppy, stuttering playback
- ❌ Distorted, too-quiet, or silent audio
- ❌ Quality regressions discovered days later, from a customer complaint — with no record of when they started
- ❌ No objective way to compare one device model, batch or software version against another

---

## The VirtualPyTest Solution

✅ **Automatic, continuous scoring** — every monitored device is graded on picture and sound quality, every minute, with no extra setup per test
✅ **One quality score per device** — a simple, at-a-glance health indicator instead of raw technical numbers
✅ **History you can scrub through** — a 24-hour quality timeline next to the live stream, so you can jump straight to the exact moment something went wrong
✅ **Trends over time** — track quality per device, per model, or per software version across days and weeks
✅ **Objective evidence** — hard numbers to settle a "was it actually bad?" conversation with a partner or vendor

---

## What Gets Measured

**Picture quality** — sharpness, blockiness/pixelation, and how smoothly video plays back.

**Sound quality** — loudness, distortion, and unexpected silence.

Both are combined into a single, easy-to-read quality score per device, refreshed every minute.

> These are fast, consistent automated measurements built for catching trends and regressions early — not a certified broadcast-lab probe. Think "an always-on quality watchdog," not "a lab report."

---

## Where to Find It

- **Fleet Dashboard** — a quality indicator next to every device, so a problem stands out immediately
- **Device Detail View** — the live stream with a synchronized 24-hour quality timeline underneath; scrub back to the exact minute an issue occurred
- **Analytics Dashboards** — fleet-wide quality trends over time, filterable by device, model or software version

---

## How It Helps

- Catch a quality regression the moment a new firmware or app release rolls out — before customers notice
- Compare device models, hardware batches, or software versions side by side on objective quality numbers
- Correlate a quality dip with a specific test run, to tell "the device glitched" from "the test caused it"
- Back up a quality complaint (or dismiss one) with data instead of opinion
