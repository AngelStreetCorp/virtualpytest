# Audio & Video Quality Monitoring

**Not just "is it on", but "does it look and sound good".**

VirtualPyTest scores the picture and sound of every monitored device, all the time. Quality problems are caught automatically, before a customer has to complain.

---

## Why it matters

A device can be on and still show a bad picture or sound.

<div data-technical>

- Blurry, blocky or pixelated video, which a plain uptime check does not notice.
- Choppy, stuttering playback.
- Distorted, too quiet, or silent audio.
- Quality drops that are found days later, from a complaint, with no record of when they started.
- No fair way to compare one device model, batch or software version with another.

</div>

---

## What you get

- **Automatic scoring.** Every monitored device is graded every minute.
- **One score per device.** A single health indicator you can read at a glance.
- **History you can scroll back through.** A 24-hour quality timeline sits next to the live stream.

<div data-technical>

- **Trends over time.** Follow quality per device, per model or per software version, over days and weeks.
- **Hard evidence.** Real numbers settle the question "was it really bad?" with a partner or vendor.

</div>

---

## What gets measured

**Picture quality.** How sharp the image is, whether it is blocky or pixelated, and how smoothly the video plays.

**Sound quality.** Loudness, distortion, and unexpected silence.

Both feed one score per device, refreshed every minute.

> These are fast automated checks that catch trends early. They are not a certified broadcast-lab test.

---

## Where to find it

- **Fleet Dashboard.** A quality indicator beside every device.
- **Device Detail View.** The live stream, with a 24-hour quality timeline underneath.
- **Analytics Dashboards.** Fleet-wide quality trends, filtered by device, model or software version.

---

## What it helps you do

- Catch a quality drop soon after a new firmware or app release.
- Compare device models, hardware batches or software versions side by side.

<div data-technical>

- Tell "the device glitched" from "the test caused it", by matching a quality dip to a test run.
- Back up a quality complaint, or dismiss it, with data instead of opinion.

</div>
